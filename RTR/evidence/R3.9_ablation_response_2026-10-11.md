## Reviewer comment (verbatim)

> Parameter robustness and ablation analyses are needed. HSeeker combines several algorithmic
> components: mirror-repeat detection, purity constraints, mismatch tolerance, AT-content filtering,
> homopolymer exclusion, overlap removal, pairing energies, stacking contributions, mismatch
> penalties and boundary optimization. It is presently difficult to determine which components
> generate the reported performance improvement. The authors should provide an ablation analysis
> comparing, for example, sequence detection alone; detection plus composition filtering;
> pairing-only scoring; pairing plus stacking; and the complete model. Similarly, performance across
> reasonable purity, mismatch, minimum-arm and spacer thresholds should be reported. This would
> demonstrate that the results are not dependent on one hand-selected parameterization.

## Response text

We thank the reviewer for this suggestion. We added a reproducible ablation and parameter analysis
on the expanded, balanced benchmark (130 records: 65 forming and 65 non-forming, of which 70 are
experimental and 60 are designed negatives). A record is called H-DNA-forming when at least one
candidate survives; no score threshold is used. All settings other than the component being varied
are the defaults (minimum arm length 8, maximum spacer 10, purity 0.90, mismatch tolerance 0.10, AT
threshold 0.80, homopolymer filtering on).

**Component ablation.** Mirror-repeat detection alone (no purity requirement, no filters) detected
63 of 65 forming records and 7 of 65 non-forming records (sensitivity 0.969, 95 % CI 0.895–0.992;
specificity 0.892, 0.794–0.947; MCC 0.864). The seven false positives were the experimentally
non-forming c-MYC variant B-33, one G-quadruplex, two Z-DNA (CG)n repeats, and three perfect mirror
repeats with balanced base composition, that is, the controls designed to show that mirror symmetry
alone is not sufficient. Adding the purity requirement removed all seven (specificity 1.000,
0.944–1.000; MCC 0.970) without losing a forming record. Adding the AT-content filter, pairing-only
scoring, pairing plus stacking, and the complete model (boundary optimisation and homopolymer
filtering) changed no record-level call. Pairing-only and pairing-plus-stacking scores rank the same
candidates, and once purity is applied no non-forming record has any candidate, so the exploratory
score AUROC (0.985) only restates the detection result. On the 70 experimental records alone, the
same stages give MCC 0.708 for detection alone and 0.832 from the purity step onward. These are
within-cohort descriptions, not independent estimates of predictive accuracy. The scorer does not
expose an independent mismatch-penalty switch, so we do not claim to have ablated that penalty
separately.

**Overlap handling.** Retaining all candidates, greedy selection and score-based selection gave
identical record-level calls (63/65 forming, 0/65 non-forming).

**Parameter variation, one at a time.** With the other settings at their defaults:
- purity 0.80–0.90 gave MCC 0.970, while 0.95 and 1.00 lost 9 forming records (MCC 0.843);
- mismatch tolerance 0 or 0.05 detected 49/65 forming records (MCC 0.778), 0.10 detected 63/65
  (0.970), and 0.15 or 0.20 detected 64/65 but added one false positive (0.969);
- minimum arm length 6 added 10 false positives (MCC 0.839), 7 and 8 gave 0.985 and 0.970,
  9–10 lost 8 forming records (0.884), and 12 lost 16 (0.778);
- maximum spacer 3 detected 41/65 forming records (MCC 0.679), 5 detected 55/65 (0.856), and
  8–15 detected 63/65 (0.970).

The single record that minimum arm 7 (and mismatch 0.15) gains is a tandem direct repeat,
(ACCCTCCCC)4, which is outside the mirror-repeat model; minimum arm 6 adds ten false positives.

**Joint variation and selection out of sample.** We evaluated all 576 combinations of purity
(0.85–1.00), mismatch (0–0.15), minimum arm (8, 10, 12), maximum spacer (5, 10, 15) and the four
filter states, with selection inside the training folds only. Under nested cross-validation (5 folds,
20 repeats), leave-one-family-out and leave-one-study-out validation, the fixed default
configuration (MCC 0.970) performed as well as or better than per-fold tuning (0.939, 0.924 and
0.924). Minimum arm 8 was selected in 99 % of nested folds and in every family and study fold, and
mismatch 0.10 in 48 of 52 family folds and 24 of 27 study folds. Performance therefore depends on
the parameters as described above, but the reported results do not rest on one hand-picked
parameterisation; we report the settings and their trade-offs rather than claim a universally
optimal configuration. The analysis code, the record-level calls and all tables are in
`analysis/hseeker_ablation_comment9.ipynb`, `analysis/scripts/run_ablation_comment9.py`,
`analysis/results/ablation_comment9/` and `analysis/results/robustness_v3/`.
